Hamiltonian 2-lift of a regular graph

Input: a graph in graph6 format or as an adjacency list (one line per vertex, e.g. 1: 2 3 4).
Output: a 2-lift H of G (2n vertices, same degrees, girth ≥ girth of G) together with a Hamiltonian cycle of H.

In the lift every vertex v has two copies v and v'. An edge uv is either parallel (u-v, u'-v') or cross (u-v', u'-v).

Algorithm:

  1. Find a perfect matching M and a 2-factor F disjoint from M such that F + M is connected (for cubic graphs F = E(G) - M).
  2. Double the edges of M and take an Euler tour of F + 2M. Every vertex has degree 4, so the tour visits every vertex twice.
  3. If the tour goes x, m(x), x along both copies of the matching edge (m(x) is the partner of x), reverse the part of the tour between the two visits of m(x). Repeat until there are no such returns.
  4. Start the tour with an M-edge. The first visit of a vertex gets layer 0, the second visit layer 1.
  5. An edge is cross if the layer changes along it, otherwise it is parallel.

Then (w1, layer1), ..., (w2n, layer2n) is a Hamiltonian cycle of H.
