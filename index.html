Hamiltonian 2-lift of a regular graph

This page takes a connected k-regular graph G and builds a graph H with twice as many vertices, the same degrees and girth at least the girth of G, such that H has a Hamiltonian cycle. Every step is shown the way you would do it by hand.

H is a 2-lift of G: every vertex v has two copies, v (copy 0) and v' (copy 1). Every edge uv is either parallel (u-v and u'-v') or cross (u-v' and u'-v). We only have to decide which edges are cross.

Main idea: a Hamiltonian cycle of H is the same thing as a closed walk in G that visits every vertex twice. The first visit of a vertex is taken in copy 0, the second visit in copy 1, and an edge is cross exactly where the walk switches copies.
